How to play

Every day there's a new number. Find the prime closest to it.

Drag the ball along the number line and drop it on your guess. The farther you drag from today's number, the more the line stretches out to show.

You get 3 tries. Each drop leaves a colored marker showing how close you were to the closest prime:

  • Correct
  • Hot: 1–2 away
  • Warm: 3–5 away
  • Cool: 6–10 away
  • Cold: more than 10 away

From your second try on, you'll also see whether you got warmer or colder.

A prime is a whole number greater than 1 that only divides by 1 and itself, like 2, 3, 5, 7, 11. Today's number is never prime. If two primes are equally close, either one counts.
